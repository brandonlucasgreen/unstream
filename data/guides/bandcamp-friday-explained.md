# Bandcamp Friday, explained

On the **first Friday of every month**, Bandcamp waives their revenue share on all sales. Normally they take 10–15% (which is already pretty good). On Bandcamp Fridays, that drops to zero.

Artists take home about **97%** of every sale — the only deduction is credit card processing fees. It's about as close as you can get to handing someone cash.

## How it started

Bandcamp Friday launched in **March 2020** when COVID shut down live music overnight. Bandcamp waived their fees for a day to push sales directly to artists. The first one did $4.3 million in a single day.

It stuck. It's now a permanent monthly event, and the music community treats it like a holiday for buying music.

## The math

On a normal day, a $10 album on Bandcamp nets the artist about **$8.00–8.50** after Bandcamp's cut and payment processing. On Bandcamp Friday, that same purchase nets about **$9.40–9.70**.

The per-transaction difference is small. But Bandcamp Fridays concentrate a huge amount of buying into one day — artists promote their catalogs, fans set aside budget, and the whole thing becomes a collective event. Way more total sales than a typical Friday.

For reference: an artist would need roughly 2,500 Spotify streams to earn what one $10 Bandcamp Friday purchase gives them.

## Getting the most out of it

**Build a wishlist.** Throughout the month, when you hear something you like, add it to your Bandcamp wishlist. When Bandcamp Friday comes around, you just work through the list instead of scrambling.

**Set a budget.** Even $10–15/month is meaningful. Over a year that's 12+ albums you own, with nearly all the money going to artists.

**Look for specials.** Artists and labels often run Bandcamp Friday promotions — exclusive releases, discount codes, bundle deals, new drops timed for the date. Worth checking their social media in the days before.

**Buy from smaller artists.** The artists who benefit most are independent and unsigned musicians who rely on direct sales. Your $10 means a lot more to someone with 500 fans than someone with 500,000.

**Share what you buy.** Post about it, tell friends, leave a comment on the Bandcamp page. Word of mouth is still the best discovery tool in music.

## Unstream on Bandcamp Fridays

[Unstream](https://unstream.stream) flags Bandcamp Friday automatically — search results show the adjusted ~97% payout for Bandcamp listings and a "BC Friday!" label. Quick way to check if an artist you're thinking about is on Bandcamp.

Bandcamp Friday is the first Friday of every month. [isitbandcampfriday.com](https://isitbandcampfriday.com) will tell you if today's the day.
