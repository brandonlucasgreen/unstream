The story of the week is not a Spotify rate tweak or another Suno rumor. It's that a major label bought a piece of the rails most "indie" distribution rides on, while two artist-financing products launched within seven days. Independence is increasingly a credit product.

## Platform watch

- **Bandcamp**: No product news. Bandcamp Friday remains on the May 1 schedule (eight Fridays for 2026, set in December). Thirty months into Songtradr ownership, Bandcamp has shipped no meaningful change to the artist payout split or core feature set. The silence is the news. [Music Ally](https://musically.com/2025/12/08/bandcamp-sets-dates-for-eight-bandcamp-friday-promos-in-2026/)
- **Patreon**: 2025 transparency report dropped this week alongside the disclosure that podcasters earned **$629M on Patreon in 2025 (+33% YoY)**. Music creators are not broken out — they remain the underrepresented sibling to podcasts on the platform. Patreon's on-platform discovery push is the more interesting development to watch for musicians, since it could mute the "drive your own audience" tax. [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/digital/news/patreon-podcaster-earnings-2025-biggest-content-category-1236710779/) | [Digital Music News](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2026/04/08/patreon-podcast-earnings-2025/)
- **Beatport / Qobuz**: Both quiet on policy. Beatport's "Next Class of 2026" is rolling out across editorial channels; Qobuz is at Brick Lane Jazz Festival capturing another live album. Hi-res and curation positioning, no payout movement.

## Emerging & alternative

- **Subvert.fm**: The co-op's **AI Policy** — drafted over months by elected artist-member board reps Hannah Lee Benson and Iz Ocampo — is now live. Headline rule: no AI-generated music on the platform, with an explicit position that training on co-op members' catalogs without consent is theft. This is the most concrete co-op-governed AI policy in music to date. Compare to Bandcamp, which has no public AI stance. Members-only alpha continues to expand through 2026. [Subvert blog](https://subvert.fm/blog/opening-up-subvert-an-update/)
- **OpenWav** + **TuneCore**: Two artist-financing products launched in the same week. **OpenWav Artist Bank** (April 2, Snafu-backed) and **TuneCore Direct Advance** (April 9, in partnership with RoyFi) both offer cash advances against future streaming royalties. Useful liquidity, but the trade is a slice of future income — independence financialized into a yield product. Worth watching whether artists actually take up these products at scale or whether they flow mostly to those who'd otherwise sign label deals. [MBW: OpenWav](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/wyclef-jean-and-jaeson-mas-d2c-service-openwav-launches-snafu-backed-indie-artist-financing-platform/) | [MBW: TuneCore/RoyFi](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/tunecore-partners-with-royfi-to-offer-royalty-advances-to-indie-artists/)
- **Mirlo, Ampwall, Faircamp, Jam.coop, Bandwagon, EVEN**: No news this week. Mirlo's public update cadence has slowed since December 2025 — worth a check-in.

## Fediverse & decentralized

- **Funkwhale**: Versions through **v0.60.3** have shipped this spring, adding Jukebox mode, smarter playlists, and proper multi-disk album handling. Modest but the most substantive Funkwhale activity in roughly two years. The April 1 NLnet ideas deadline passed; whether a funded grant follows is the more important signal. [Funkwhale docs](https://docs.funkwhale.audio/)
- **Nina Protocol / Sound.xyz**: Quiet week. Nina remains the cleanest "100% to artist" pitch in on-chain music; Sound continues its curated-drop model. No new signals worth alerting on.

## Streaming economics

- **Spotify**: Spotify pushed an April 2026 per-stream rate adjustment, framed in its newsroom as "more artist-forward." Secondary trackers land the effective rate near **$0.00318** — still inside the $0.003–$0.005 band that has held for years. The 1,000-stream-per-rolling-12-months minimum (live since April 2024) remains in place, which means the rate change does nothing for most artists. Read the framing carefully; the math is essentially unchanged for the long tail. [Chartlex](https://www.chartlex.com/blog/money/how-much-does-spotify-pay-per-stream-2026) | [Spotify newsroom](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-01-28/2025-music-industry-payouts-whats-next-for-artists/)
- **Concentration**: Axios this week confirmed what indie circles already knew — streaming payouts remain dominated by the top tier even as the absolute number of paid creators grows. Per CelebrityAccess on the latest IFPI numbers, streaming is now **70% of recorded-music revenue**. The pie is bigger; the slice for the bottom 99% is not. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/media-trends-membership/2026/04/04/streaming-music-payout-gap) | [CelebrityAccess](https://celebrityaccess.com/2026/04/01/173729)

## Industry pulse

- **Warner Music Group → Revelator**: WMG signed a definitive agreement on April 1 to acquire **Revelator** — the B2B distribution, rights-management, and royalty-accounting platform that powers a substantial slice of indie infrastructure. This is the more important ownership story of the month than anything happening at consumer-facing platforms. A major now owns rails that thousands of indie distributors and labels run on. Watch for downstream impact on any indie distributor or platform integrating Revelator. [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/warner-music-group-agrees-to-acquire-revelator-state-of-the-art-independent-music-platform-302731480.html)
- **Suno × labels**: Suno's licensing talks with UMG and Sony have stalled, reportedly over whether users can download AI-generated tracks. WMG already settled with both Suno and Udio in late 2025 (Udio's deal forces a pivot to a "walled garden" remix platform; Suno's allows broader output). Sony's cases against both remain active. A pivotal fair-use ruling is expected summer 2026 — the most consequential AI-music legal moment of the year. [Winbuzzer](https://winbuzzer.com/2026/04/09/suno-licensing-talks-with-labels-stall-over-ai-music-downloa-xcxwbn/) | [MBW](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/sunos-licensing-talks-with-major-labels-in-limbo-with-no-path-forward-report/)
- **Musixmatch Sentinel**: New service claiming millisecond-latency detection of copyrighted music or lyrics inside AI-generated content. The detection-as-a-service layer aimed at platforms (not labels) is the interesting move; the latency claim is unverified. [The Business Tempo](https://www.xavierboscher.com/en/the-business-tempo-april-2026-streaming-ai-and-copyright-the-music-industry-at-a-crossroads/)

## Unstream implications

Concrete actions for this week:

1. **Surface platform AI policy as a comparison axis.** Unstream already shows artist payout split per platform. Subvert just demonstrated that *AI policy* is a meaningful axis fans care about. Add a one-line AI stance per platform where one exists publicly (Subvert: explicit no-AI; Bandcamp: silent; Spotify: opaque). Low-effort UX, on-thesis.
2. **Audit which "indie" links route through Revelator.** If WMG owns Revelator, Unstream's users deserve to know which independent-looking distribution paths ride a major-label-owned rail. Concrete task: identify which indie distributors integrated in our search sources use Revelator infrastructure and decide whether that warrants a label.
3. **Skip OpenWav Artist Bank and TuneCore Direct Advance integrations.** Financing products, not artist destinations. Out of scope.
4. **Reach out to Mirlo.** Public update cadence has slowed. Worth knowing whether the cooperative is funded through 2026 before continuing to recommend them as heavily as we do.

## Confidence

- **Confirmed**: WMG-Revelator agreement (PR Newswire); OpenWav Artist Bank launch April 2 (MBW); TuneCore/RoyFi launch April 9 (MBW); Patreon $629M podcaster figure (Variety + DMN); Suno-labels talks stalled (Winbuzzer + MBW); Funkwhale v0.60.3 (project docs).
- **Reported, not independently verified**: The exact $0.00318 Spotify per-stream figure is from secondary trackers; Spotify's own newsroom frames the change differently. Treat the headline number as approximate.
- **Speculative**: Outcome of Sony's AI cases. A summer 2026 fair-use ruling is widely anticipated but not formally scheduled.
- **Unverified**: Musixmatch Sentinel's millisecond-latency detection claim is vendor-sourced.
