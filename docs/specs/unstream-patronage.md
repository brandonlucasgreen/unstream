# Unstream Patronage

Tiers:
- monthly/yearly pass: $2/mo or $20/yr
- Patron pass: $5+

Goal: 
- Create a means for music fans to directly support their favorite artists with little friction or stakes. 
- Expand payout options for artists. 
- Create a small revenue stream for maintaining Unstream itself

Requirements:
- To participate, artists need to verify themselves, set up a profile, and connect a stripe account. This allows the artist to receive direct support through Unstream
- Listeners can subscribe optionally ￼to an upstream monthly or yearly pass. This gives them the ability to support any number of artists eligible for patronage. The listener can customize their past payments, but it must be a minimum of five dollars monthly or $20 annually. 
- Once the listener is subscribed, they can choose any number of eligible artists To which there patronage payment will be divided, the platform takes a 5% fee   (capped at $5) per transaction. The percentage cut allows upstream to continue staying alive, but the cap is to avoid the platform taking too much away from artists. 
- Transparency is critical for the whole thing to work. If a listener decides to subscribe for $10 a month, they should see exactly how much each player receives of that $10 payment: How much goes to each individual artist, how much goes to unstream, and how much is set aside for processing fees. Similarly, the artist should have a transparent payouts screen as part of their dashboard, making clear how much they make each month or year, and among how many patrons.
