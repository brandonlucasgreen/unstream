### 3. Cold pitch email (backup)

**Subject:** Open-source tool that shows music fans where their money actually goes

Hi [Writer name],

I built a free tool called **Unstream** (https://unstream.stream) that searches 13+ platforms to show fans where any artist sells music — and what percentage actually goes to the artist on each one.

The premise is that most of the infrastructure for supporting artists directly already exists (Bandcamp, Mirlo, Faircamp, Qobuz, library services, etc.) — it's just fragmented enough that most fans never find it. Unstream puts it in one place, with payout transparency baked in. It works as a web app, browser extensions for Chrome and Firefox, and a macOS menu bar app.

The whole thing is free and open source. No premium tier, no data collection. I'm a musician who built it because I wanted to know where my own money was going.

Happy to share more if this is up your alley.

Thanks!
Brandon
