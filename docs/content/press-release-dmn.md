**Independent Musician Launches Free Tool to Help Fans Support Artists Beyond Streaming**

*Unstream searches 13+ platforms to find where artists sell music directly, with transparent payout data*

**March 2026** — Independent musician Brandon Lucas Green has launched Unstream (https://unstream.stream), a free tool that helps music fans find where any artist sells, shares, or accepts support for their music across 13+ platforms outside the streaming ecosystem — making it easier for listeners to reduce their dependency on streaming apps.

Unstream searches platforms including Bandcamp, Mirlo, Ampwall, Faircamp, Qobuz, Patreon, Ko-fi, and library services like Hoopla and Freegal. Results are grouped by category — music marketplaces, patronage, decentralized platforms, and library services — so fans can quickly find the option that works for them. Each result also displays artist payout percentages, so fans can see how much of their money actually reaches the artist.

The tool highlights Bandcamp Fridays, the monthly event where Bandcamp waives its revenue share and artists keep even more from each sale.

Unstream is available as a web app, browser extensions for Chrome and Firefox that surface alternatives while users browse Spotify or YouTube, and a macOS menu bar app that detects currently-playing tracks and shows where to support that artist directly.

The entire tool is free, open source, and collects no listener data. There is no premium tier or paywall.

"Most of the infrastructure for supporting artists directly already exists — Bandcamp, Mirlo, Faircamp, library services — it's just scattered across platforms that most fans don't know about," said Green, who has earned over $2,000 from Bandcamp sales over two years as an artist despite a small following and no live presence. "The whole point is making it easy for fans to find these places, and to get more money to artists."

**About Unstream**
Unstream is a free, open-source music discovery tool that helps fans find and support artists on alternative platforms. It searches 13+ platforms, displays payout transparency data, and is available on web, Chrome, Firefox, and macOS. Learn more at https://unstream.stream.
